## Cross-cutting findings (for the parallel build subagents)

1. **Example-data everywhere, one real API.** Of the six dashboards, only `/teacher-dashboard` calls a real Supabase-backed route. Everything else renders `EXAMPLE_*` constants from `src/lib/example-data.ts` (10 exports: `EXAMPLE_CASE`, `EXAMPLE_PROVIDER`, `EXAMPLE_ATTORNEY_PROVIDER`, `EXAMPLE_CHECKLIST`, `EXAMPLE_ACTION_ITEMS`, `EXAMPLE_CHAT`, `EXAMPLE_VERIFICATIONS`, `EXAMPLE_USCIS`, `EXAMPLE_PRIORITY_ACTIONS`, `EXAMPLE_SMART_FORMS`, `EXAMPLE_TRANSLATIONS`, `EXAMPLE_CHAT_UPGRADED`) or local `useState`. Do not mistake a rendered section for a wired feature.
2. **No `middleware.ts`, no dashboard session gating** (except teacher). Any paywall or role gate added to a page must also be enforced in the API route it calls — today neither layer enforces payment.
3. **`tierFeatures()` does not exist** (`src/lib/permissions.ts:13` cites it; `src/lib/serviceTiers.ts` has no such export and no caller). Any builder relying on tier gating must implement it; do not assume it exists.
4. **Two intake engines, two preparer back offices, one shared legal page.** `DynamicFormIntakeEngine.tsx` (root `components/`, registry 15) vs `components/portal/DynamicFormIntakeEngine.tsx` (4-form); `/back-office` vs `/company-back-office` vs `/preparer-dashboard` (three preparer surfaces); `/legal-back-office` is one page for three roles. Pick canonical homes before building on any of them.
5. **Notify-stage is built but orphaned.** The engine + templates + SES email path are the most complete "exists" in this audit after SES itself — but with zero callers it sends nothing. Wiring a stage-change call site is a smaller job than building a notifier.
6. **Prices untouched.** Locked prices (bundles $999/$1,799/$2,799, DIY $25, Review $99, Member $9.80/mo or $98/yr, Preparer $49/mo, Attorney $99/mo, DOJ $24.50/$49.50/mo, Law Student $35/mo) were not changed and were not re-audited here beyond the carry-forward from AUDIT-2026-11-24.md §3a.
