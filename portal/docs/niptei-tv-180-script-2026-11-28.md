# Niptei TV — 180-Second Video Script + Storyboard
## Immigration with NIPTEI: for clients AND for professionals

**Status: VIDEO SCRIPT ONLY — not a finished video. NOT posted. NOT produced.**
No video was generated in this build. This is a script and storyboard for
production, in the same pattern as the six 60-second interview-prep scripts
in `src/lib/interviewPrep.ts` (`VIDEO_SCRIPTS`).

**Website (big, top and bottom, throughout — founder's standing video rule):**
`immigration.niptei.com` — shown big and fully readable at the top AND the
bottom of the screen in EVERY scene, start to end. 9:16 vertical.

---

## ⚠️ LENGTH CONFLICT — FOUNDER DECISION NEEDED BEFORE PRODUCTION

The founder's standing Niptei TV rule is:

- **60 seconds per video**, 9:16 vertical, website name big top and bottom.
- **Post once a week, all five together** (one video per business).

This request is for a **180-second video** — three times the standing length,
for one business only. That conflicts with the standing rule on two counts:

1. **Length:** 180 seconds vs. the standing 60 seconds.
2. **Posting:** a single 180-second video vs. posting once a week, all five
   together.

**This build resolves the conflict by building the 180-second SCRIPT ONLY.**
It does NOT generate the finished video, does NOT post anything, and does NOT
change the standing rule. Before anyone produces or posts this video, the
founder must decide one of:

- (a) Approve a one-off 180-second video as an exception to the 60-second rule; or
- (b) Cut this script into three 60-second videos (Scenes 1–4, 5–8, 9–12
  below are written so each block stands alone as a 60-second video); or
- (c) Do not produce it.

**Do not produce or post until the founder picks (a), (b), or (c).**

## The video at a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Title** | "Your Whole Immigration Journey, In One Place — And the Back Office Behind It" |
| **Length** | 180 seconds (12 scenes × 15 seconds) |
| **Format** | 9:16 vertical — `immigration.niptei.com` big, top and bottom, every scene |
| **Audience 1** | People who need immigration services (clients and families) |
| **Audience 2** | Attorneys, DOJ-accredited representatives, preparers, nonprofits — professionals who want the platform as their back office |
| **Promise** | None about outcomes. The video never promises approval, a visa, or a date. It promises a clear process, honest prices, and help in your language. |
| **Languages on screen** | English on screen; narration notes where the platform works in 7 languages (English, Spanish, Haitian Creole, French, Portuguese, Chinese, Arabic) |

**Honesty rules for production (do not drop):**

- Immigration with NIPTEI is a **document-preparation service, NOT a law
  firm, NOT USCIS affiliated**. Only a licensed attorney or DOJ-accredited
  representative gives legal advice (8 CFR 292.1). The video says this on
  screen in Scene 11.
- **No approval is promised or implied.** No "get your green card" claim.
  The words used are "prepare", "track", "practise", "help".
- **No invented clients, professionals, reviews, prices, or rankings.**
  Every price named on screen is a locked price from
  `src/lib/stripePrices.ts` (DIY $25 per form; Member $9.80/mo — see Scenes
  4 and 9). No person on screen is presented as a real client or a real
  professional unless they really are one.
- The interview practice questions shown are **practice questions, not
  official USCIS questions** — except the civics questions, which are
  official USCIS questions (see Scene 7 note).

## Scene-by-scene script (12 scenes × 15 seconds)

### Scene 1 — 0:00–0:15 — The problem: lost in the paperwork
- **Visual:** A kitchen table covered in papers — forms, letters, a passport,
  a phone showing a USCIS letter. A woman looks at the pile, overwhelmed.
  Website name big, top and bottom.
- **On-screen text:** `IMMIGRATION PAPERWORK SHOULDN'T FEEL LIKE THIS`
- **Narration:** "Forms. Letters. Deadlines. A whole life in a pile of paper —
  and no one telling you what comes next."
- **Website placement:** `immigration.niptei.com` — big, top and bottom.

### Scene 2 — 0:15–0:30 — One journey map, your whole case
- **Visual:** The same woman opens her phone. One clean page: her Journey Map
  — where she started, where she is now, what is next, what is missing.
  Website name big, top and bottom.
- **On-screen text:** `YOUR JOURNEY MAP — WHERE YOU ARE, WHAT'S NEXT`
- **Narration:** "Immigration with NIPTEI gives you one map for your whole
  journey — where you started, where you are now, and what is next. In plain
  words. In your language."
- **Website placement:** `immigration.niptei.com` — big, top and bottom.

### Scene 3 — 0:30–0:45 — The whole family, one case
- **Visual:** The woman taps "Family". Her husband abroad and her mother
  appear as part of the same case — each with their own login, each seeing
  only their part. Website name big, top and bottom.
- **On-screen text:** `ONE CASE FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY`
- **Narration:** "Your whole family in one case. Your husband abroad, your
  mother, your sponsor — each with their own login, each seeing only their
  part. Nobody sees what is not theirs."
- **Website placement:** `immigration.niptei.com` — big, top and bottom.

### Scene 4 — 0:45–1:00 — Honest prices, before you pay (end of part 1)
- **Visual:** The phone shows the flat prices: DIY $25 per form, Member
  $9.80 a month. A free Packet Health Check runs first — it shows what is
  wrong or missing BEFORE any payment. Website name big, top and bottom.
- **On-screen text:** `DIY $25 A FORM • MEMBER $9.80 A MONTH — FREE CHECK FIRST`
- **Narration:** "And the price is the price. Do it yourself for twenty-five
  dollars a form. And before you pay anything, a free check shows you what is
  wrong or missing."
- **Website placement:** `immigration.niptei.com` — big, top and bottom.
- **Cut point:** Scenes 1–4 stand alone as a 60-second video ("Your journey,
  your family, your price") if the founder picks option (b).

### Scene 5 — 1:00–1:15 — Your documents, safe — and sent again when you lose them
- **Visual:** A vault on the phone: passport, birth certificate, tax returns,
  each with an expiry date. A reminder pops up: "Your passport expires in 60
  days." Then: "Document sent again to your email." Website name big, top
  and bottom.
- **On-screen text:** `YOUR VAULT — EXPIRY REMINDERS • RESEND ANYTIME`
- **Narration:** "Your documents live in your vault. We remind you before
  anything expires — and if you lose a copy, we send it again, to your email
  or your phone."
- **Website placement:** `immigration.niptei.com` — big, top and bottom.

### Scene 6 — 1:15–1:30 — Forms that follow each other, and news that follows your case
- **Visual:** Forms chain together on screen: I-130 → I-485 → work permit.
  Then a news alert: "A rule changed for I-485 cases — here is what it means
  for yours." Website name big, top and bottom.
- **On-screen text:** `FORMS THAT FOLLOW EACH OTHER • NEWS THAT FOLLOWS YOUR CASE`
- **Narration:** "Your forms follow each other — one finished form starts the
  next. And when the news changes, you hear about it only if it touches YOUR
  case."
- **Website placement:** `immigration.niptei.com` — big, top and bottom.

### Scene 7 — 1:30–1:45 — Practise the interview before the interview
- **Visual:** The woman practises on her phone: a civics question, a timer,
  a plain-English score — "Getting there — practise these three." A small
  line under the score: "Practice only. This does not predict your result."
  Website name big, top and bottom.
- **On-screen text:** `MOCK INTERVIEW WITH A SCORE — PRACTICE, NEVER A PREDICTION`
- **Narration:** "Practise the interview before the interview — timed, with a
  score in plain English and what to improve. Practice only. It never
  predicts your result."
- **Website placement:** `immigration.niptei.com` — big, top and bottom.
- **Production note:** the civics question shown must be one of the official
  USCIS 100 questions; the personal questions shown must be labelled on
  screen as practice questions, not official ones.

### Scene 8 — 1:45–2:00 — Every day, one small step (end of part 2)
- **Visual:** Morning. The phone's Today screen: one next step, one interview
  question of the day, one piece of news. A checkmark as she finishes the
  step. Website name big, top and bottom.
- **On-screen text:** `TODAY: ONE STEP • ONE QUESTION • ONE UPDATE`
- **Narration:** "And every morning, one small step. One question to practise.
  One update that matters. That is how a long journey gets finished."
- **Website placement:** `immigration.niptei.com` — big, top and bottom.
- **Cut point:** Scenes 5–8 stand alone as a 60-second video ("Your vault,
  your forms, your interview") if the founder picks option (b).

### Scene 9 — 2:00–2:15 — Now, for the professionals: your back office
- **Visual:** Cut to a desk: an attorney (actor, labelled as such if shown)
  opens a dashboard — her clients, their stages, renewals due, fees. The
  same platform, the professional side. Website name big, top and bottom.
- **On-screen text:** `FOR ATTORNEYS, PREPARERS & NONPROFITS — YOUR BACK OFFICE`
- **Narration:** "And if you are the professional — an attorney, a preparer,
  a nonprofit — this is your back office. Your clients, their stages, their
  renewals, in one place."
- **Website placement:** `immigration.niptei.com` — big, top and bottom.

### Scene 10 — 2:15–2:30 — Invite, verify, work — in 7 languages
- **Visual:** The professional sends an invitation to a colleague; a
  verification badge appears ("Bar verified — listing live"). A client
  message arrives in Spanish; the professional answers in English and the
  client reads it in Spanish. Website name big, top and bottom.
- **On-screen text:** `INVITE YOUR TEAM • VERIFIED LISTING • 7 LANGUAGES`
- **Narration:** "Invite your team. Get verified before you list — bar,
  DOJ roster, supervisor — checked first. And talk to every client in their
  language. Seven languages, end to end."
- **Website placement:** `immigration.niptei.com` — big, top and bottom.

### Scene 11 — 2:30–2:45 — The honest line (must stay in the video)
- **Visual:** A clean, quiet screen. No actors, no pile of paper — just the
  words, big and readable. Website name big, top and bottom.
- **On-screen text:** `WE PREPARE DOCUMENTS. WE ARE NOT A LAW FIRM. WE ARE NOT USCIS. ONLY A LICENSED ATTORNEY OR DOJ-ACCREDITED REPRESENTATIVE GIVES LEGAL ADVICE. NO ONE CAN PROMISE AN OUTCOME.`
- **Narration:** "We prepare documents. We are not a law firm, and we are not
  USCIS. Only a licensed attorney or a DOJ-accredited representative gives
  legal advice — and no one, not us, not anyone, can promise an outcome."
- **Website placement:** `immigration.niptei.com` — big, top and bottom.
- **Production note:** this scene must not be cut, shortened, or moved to
  small print. If the video is cut into three 60-second videos, this scene
  (or its first two sentences) must appear in each part.

### Scene 12 — 2:45–3:00 — The ask: register today
- **Visual:** Split screen: the woman at her kitchen table, smiling, phone in
  hand — and the professional at her desk, dashboard open. Both screens show
  the same thing: the registration page. The website name fills the top and
  bottom, bigger than anywhere else. Website name big, top and bottom.
- **On-screen text:** `START YOUR JOURNEY — OR RUN YOUR PRACTICE HERE` / `REGISTER TODAY` / `immigration.niptei.com`
- **Narration:** "Start your journey — or run your practice here. Register
  today at immigration.niptei.com."
- **Website placement:** `immigration.niptei.com` — big, top and bottom,
